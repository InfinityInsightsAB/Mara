\section{Direct prices for the numerical experiments}\label{app:direct-prices}
This appendix reports the direct Bermudan put prices corresponding to the numerical experiments in Section~\ref{sec:numerical}. All price entries are rounded to three decimal places. The label LSMC denotes the plain least-squares Monte Carlo estimator, and Hybrid denotes the Hybrid LSMC--PDE estimator.
