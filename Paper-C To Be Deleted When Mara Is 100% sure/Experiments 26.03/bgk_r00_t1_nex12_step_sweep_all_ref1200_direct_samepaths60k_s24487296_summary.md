## Main takeaway

- The strongest and most consistent Hybrid region is the middle of the step grid, especially 48 to 72 steps.
- Hybrid has much smaller SE and CI width than LSMC at the same 60,000 paths in every scenario here.
- LSMC tends to recover at the edge cases, especially 24 steps for some strikes and 96 steps for ATM and K=90.
